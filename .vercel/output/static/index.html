Our process

From the first call
to samples in hand

Same seven steps on a single-site collection or a multi-site prospective collection program.

  1. 1

    Orientation

    We take time to understand your scienctific needs, timeline, and constraints.

  2. 2

    Definition

    We develop and have you approve a detailed project spec, which drives the rest of the work.

  3. 3

    Feasibility

    We match your needs to suitable providers from our global network.

  4. 4

    Matching

    We propose selected Provier(s) to you, handle agreements, and all other logistics to start collection.

  5. 5

    Collection

    We coordinate and oversee the project from inception to collection, keeping you up-to-date of the progress.

  6. 6

    Logistics

    We arrange shipping with qualified couriers so samples arrive safely and on time.

  7. 7

    Acceptance

    You sign off when samples meet spec so we can close the project.
